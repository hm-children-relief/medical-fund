Making the Poor Kids Smile

Our foundation is dedicated to providing specialized care and pediatric support to children in need. We focus on healing, growth, and the simple joy of childhood.

About Our Good Works

The Happy Madison Children’s Relief Fund specializes in caring for children battling Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome (CPPS). We currently provide full care for over 500 children.

Our work focuses on three pillars: Specialized Pediatric Feeding, Medical Intervention, and Safe Housing. To protect the privacy of the children and homes, we do not publish specific locations, but our impact is seen in every smile.

Use of Funds

100% of your contributions are utilized for:

  • Medical Care: Specialized treatment for children with CPPS.
  • Pediatric Feeding: Specialized nutrition programs for growth.
  • Safe Shelter: Maintaining secure, private homes for recovery.

Why Gift Cards?

To maintain the absolute privacy and security of our private homes and international donors, we utilize a manual manual payment portal via verified gift cards. This allows for immediate, cross-border medical funding without the delays or public tracking associated with traditional international banking, ensuring the children receive care without interruption.
