HAPPY MADISON RELIEF FUND

Our Mission

The Happy Madison Children’s Relief Fund focuses on providing immediate medical intervention for over 500 children battling Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome (CPPS).

CPPS is a debilitating condition that requires specialized pediatric feeding programs and constant clinical monitoring. Your contributions provide medicine, nutrition, and shelter for these vulnerable children.
