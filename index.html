Happy Madison Children’s Relief Fund

Our Mission

The Happy Madison Children’s Relief Fund is a private charitable initiative dedicated to children battling Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome (CPPS). This condition is often overlooked, leaving many children in persistent physical distress without proper clinical intervention.

How We Care for Over 500 Kids

Currently, we provide 24/7 care for over 500 affected children. Your support goes directly toward:

  • Feeding Programs: Specialized pediatric nutrition required for patients.
  • Medicine: Critical pharmaceutical supplies and pain management therapies.
  • Shelter: Safe, clinical housing environments for orphaned children.

We work quietly to protect the privacy of the families and children we serve. It’s all about just trying to put a smile on their faces.
