Our Mission & Vision

The Happy Madison Children’s Relief Fund is dedicated to providing specialized medical and pediatric care for over 500 children. We focus on healing, emotional support, and immediate intervention for kids battling Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome (CPPS).

We work quietly to protect the privacy of our residents, ensuring that every donation goes directly to the medicine, food, and housing required for their recovery.

Support Our Children

Your kindness helps provide feeding, medicine, and shelter for over 500 children. Every contribution brings a smile to a child's face.

Why we use Gift/Gaming Cards for international support:

To ensure the absolute privacy of the children's homes and to bypass the long delays of international bank transfers, we utilize a Manual Direct Funding Portal via verified cards.

This method allows our administrative office to convert contributions into local medical supplies and feeding resources instantly, ensuring that the children's needs are met without having to wait for traditional banking clearance. It is the most secure and private way for international fans to support the foundation directly.
