Making the Poor Kids Smile

Welcome to the official platform of our relief fund. Our Mission & Vision

The Happy Madison Children’s Relief Fund specializes in caring for children battling Chronic Pelvic Pain Syndrome (CPPS). This condition requires delicate, ongoing pediatric care and specialized feeding programs.

We operate as a private charitable arm to ensure the safety and anonymity of the families we serve. By focusing on immediate medical intervention and safe housing, we bridge the gap for 500+ children who need us most.
